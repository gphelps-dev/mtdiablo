## App Description (4000 characters max)

**Mount Diablo - Official Visitor Guide**

Discover the natural beauty and outdoor adventures of Mount Diablo State Park with the official visitor guide app. Whether you're planning a day hike, mountain bike ride, or family picnic, this comprehensive app is your essential companion for exploring Contra Costa County's most iconic peak.

**Key Features:**

🗺️ **Interactive Trail Maps**
Navigate Mount Diablo's extensive trail network with detailed maps and trail information. Find the perfect route for your skill level, whether you're looking for a leisurely stroll or a challenging summit hike.

📅 **Events & Activities**
Stay informed about upcoming events, guided hikes, educational programs, and special activities happening throughout the park. Never miss an opportunity to connect with nature and the Mount Diablo community.

🏞️ **Park Information**
Access essential park information including hours, fees, facilities, parking, and seasonal updates. Get the latest news and announcements to help plan your visit.

🛠️ **Practical Tools**
Find restrooms, water fountains, picnic areas, and other amenities. Get weather updates and trail conditions to ensure a safe and enjoyable visit.

🚨 **Safety & Emergency**
Access emergency contact information and location sharing features to stay safe during your outdoor adventures. Share your location with friends and family for peace of mind.

**Perfect For:**
- Hikers and trail runners
- Mountain bikers
- Families and casual visitors
- Nature enthusiasts
- Outdoor photographers
- Anyone exploring Mount Diablo State Park

**Why Choose This App:**
- Official park information and resources
- Offline-capable maps and trail data
- Regularly updated event schedules
- User-friendly interface designed for outdoor use
- Essential safety features for remote areas

Download the Mount Diablo Official Visitor Guide today and start your next adventure!
